Questions

Before you scan.

What does S.C.A.L.E. actually check?
Twenty-two named checks across four measurable dimensions: whether AI crawlers can read your page, whether your role and positioning are stated plainly, whether your structured data is present and valid, and whether your profiles and citations connect back to you. The fifth dimension, momentum, is the change between scans.
Is the scan really free?
Yes. The scan needs no account. A free account saves your results, which is what lets the second scan measure momentum.
How is this different from an SEO audit?
An SEO audit measures how a page ranks in a list of links. S.C.A.L.E. measures whether an AI system can understand your business well enough to recommend it, which depends on different signals: a clearly stated identity, structured data, and corroboration from other sources.
How often should I scan?
Monthly. Scores move when you change your site or your profiles, and a monthly rhythm shows a real trend without chasing noise.
Do you store my website?
The free scan reads the public page you enter and does not keep it. With an account, results are stored privately and are visible only to you.
